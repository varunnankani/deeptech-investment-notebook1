# Research methodology

## Investment lens

The purpose of this notebook is not to prove that either company is a good company. It is to test whether each can become a venture-scale company.

### 1. Start with the customer job
What budget or workflow already exists? Is the company creating demand or changing how an existing mandatory / high-value job is performed?

### 2. Separate category validation from differentiation
Competition is not automatically negative. A competitor can prove that customers buy, that certifications can be earned, or that the technical category works. The question becomes whether the selected company still has an investable entry point and a path to differentiation.

### 3. Identify the physical bottleneck
- Octobotics: field labour / robot utilisation
- PierSight: satellite capacity / constellation capex

### 4. Define the venture-scale decoupling metric
- Octobotics: revenue and inspections grow faster than field headcount
- PierSight: recurring analytics revenue grows faster than satellite count

### 5. Write the kill criterion before writing the upside
A thesis is only useful if new evidence can invalidate it.

### 6. Treat data moats as earned, not assumed
Repeated data only becomes a moat if the company has the rights to use it, history improves the product, customers pay for the resulting intelligence, and workflows become meaningfully sticky.

# Octobotics — Working Investment Notes

## One-line thesis
Mandatory critical-infrastructure inspection is an existing budget. The investment case is whether robotics can increase inspection capacity faster than skilled field labour, with asset-health intelligence as a higher-margin second layer.

## White space
Traditional inspection can require scaffolding, work at height, confined-space entry, shutdown windows and manually sampled NDT. The wedge is not creating a new inspection budget; it is changing how an existing mandatory workflow is delivered.

## What must be true
- Hazardous-area certification creates a meaningful commercial inflection.
- Most of the robotics/autonomy/software stack is reusable across asset classes.
- Inspections per robot and revenue per field engineer rise over time.
- Repeat / multi-site customer expansion emerges.

## Competition
PetroBot is the most important Indian head-to-head. It publicly advertises ATEX/PESO certification, hundreds of inspected assets and ONGC/HPCL strategic backing. Gecko Robotics is the global scaled benchmark. Solinas is a better-funded adjacent Indian robotics player.

## Kill criteria
- Revenue scales roughly one-for-one with field headcount.
- Each new vertical requires major custom engineering.
- Certification does not unlock a larger/high-value pipeline.
- Inspection data cannot be reused sufficiently to build analytics (upside case).

## Venture-scale metric
**Revenue / field engineer** and **inspections / robot / month** should rise as deployment density and platform reuse improve.
